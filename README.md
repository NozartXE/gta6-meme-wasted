A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty: the iconic
WASTED screen, because the 13-year wait since GTA V is what killed us —
"You waited 13 years for GTA 6". make.fun's ghost stands over the scene
holding its magenta star coin: the wait finally pays. The meme picture was
drawn originally for this entry (no copied artwork), and the post's text
carries the bounty link, the play code and the pitch that people and their
AI agents earn real money on make.fun.

The post is the entry: it is posted on X from a public account, and
`POST.json` holds its address. The meme image itself lives with the entry
notes (it is posted to X, not committed here) — this repository stays
minimal: `main.ts`, `POST.json`, `README.md`, `LICENSE`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`out/POST.json` is then byte for byte this directory's `POST.json`.
